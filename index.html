WorshipGO

Church presentation software for songs, Bible verses, slides and media.

English

WorshipGO runs on the computer your church already uses. Your songs, plans and media stay on that computer.

The optional Cloud feature lets you save a service plan to your own Google Drive, so you can prepare it at home and open it on the church computer. WorshipGO only sees the files it creates itself, in a folder called WorshipGO Plans. It never reads the rest of your Drive.

See the privacy policy.

Bahasa Indonesia

WorshipGO berjalan di komputer yang sudah dipakai gereja Anda. Lagu, plan, dan media tetap tersimpan di komputer itu.

Fitur Cloud (opsional) memungkinkan Anda menyimpan plan ibadah ke Google Drive milik Anda sendiri, sehingga bisa disiapkan di rumah lalu dibuka di komputer gereja. WorshipGO hanya melihat file yang dibuatnya sendiri, di folder bernama WorshipGO Plans, dan tidak pernah membaca isi Drive Anda yang lain.

Lihat kebijakan privasi.
