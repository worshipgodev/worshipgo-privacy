WorshipGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

English

The short version: WorshipGO does not send your data to the developer. There is no WorshipGO server that receives it.

What WorshipGO stores

Songs, service plans, media and settings are stored on the computer where WorshipGO is installed. They never leave that computer unless you choose to export them or use the Cloud feature below.

Google Drive (optional Cloud feature)

  • If you sign in with Google, WorshipGO asks for three permissions: your email address (shown in Settings so you can tell which account is signed in), basic sign-in (openid), and access to files that WorshipGO itself creates in your Drive (drive.file).
  • WorshipGO uses this only to save and open your service plans in a folder called WorshipGO Plans in your own Google Drive. It cannot see your other files.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on your computer, in a file that is not included in backups or plan files. It is never sent to the developer.
  • The plan files live in your Google Drive, under your Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to them.
  • You can sign out at any time in Settings → Cloud, which also revokes WorshipGO's access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What we do not do

WorshipGO has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not receive it.

WorshipGO's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: worshipgo.dev@gmail.com

Bahasa Indonesia

Singkatnya: WorshipGO tidak mengirim data Anda ke pengembang. Tidak ada server WorshipGO yang menerimanya.

Yang disimpan WorshipGO

Lagu, plan ibadah, media, dan pengaturan disimpan di komputer tempat WorshipGO dipasang. Semuanya tidak keluar dari komputer itu, kecuali Anda sendiri mengekspornya atau memakai fitur Cloud di bawah ini.

Google Drive (fitur Cloud, opsional)

  • Bila Anda masuk dengan Google, WorshipGO meminta tiga izin: alamat email Anda (ditampilkan di Settings agar Anda tahu akun mana yang masuk), masuk dasar (openid), dan akses ke file yang dibuat WorshipGO sendiri di Drive Anda (drive.file).
  • Izin ini hanya dipakai untuk menyimpan dan membuka plan ibadah di folder WorshipGO Plans di Google Drive Anda sendiri. WorshipGO tidak bisa melihat file Anda yang lain.
  • Token masuk hanya disimpan di komputer Anda, dalam berkas yang tidak ikut backup maupun file plan, dan tidak pernah dikirim ke pengembang.
  • File plan berada di Google Drive Anda, di bawah akun Google Anda. Kebijakan privasi Google berlaku untuk file tersebut.
  • Anda bisa keluar kapan saja di Settings → Cloud, yang sekaligus mencabut akses WorshipGO. Akses juga bisa dicabut di myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Yang tidak kami lakukan

WorshipGO tidak memakai analitik, iklan, ataupun pelacakan. Kami tidak menjual atau membagikan data Anda, dan kami tidak menerimanya.

Penggunaan informasi dari Google API oleh WorshipGO mematuhi Google API Services User Data Policy, termasuk ketentuan Limited Use.

Kontak

Pertanyaan: worshipgo.dev@gmail.com
